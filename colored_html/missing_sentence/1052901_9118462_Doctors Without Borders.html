On 7 October 2023, Palestinian militant groups from the Gaza Strip invaded Israel. They launched around 3000-5000 rockets towards Israel. They attacked some of its southern cities and settlements by land, air and sea, killing hundreds of Israelis and kidnapping dozens. They also killed and kidnapped some foreigners.[12] The invasion is part of the Gaza–Israel conflict, which is part of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.

Deaths

Israel

At least 800 Israelis were killed.[13][14][15]

The police commander of Rahat, was killed.[16] The IDF said that 44 of its soldiers were killed.[17] The commander of the Nahal Brigade, Colonel Yonatan Steinberg, was killed near Kerem Shalom.[18]

In Ashkelon,[19] at least 68 died.[20] Dozens of bodies were reportedly discovered at a place, in Re'im, where there had been a party event that was part of a music festival.[21]

The Battle of Re'im happened, and there was fighting at a military base there. Colonel Roi Levy was killed in the battle.[22]

Palestinian reports state that at least 4 Israeli captives/POWs were killed by the Israeli airstrikes. Hamas announced that 19 years old POW Tomir Alon Nimrodi, ID 9212125, was killed in an Israeli airstrike on Gaza on Oct 9th, 2023. Nimrodi, originally from Kefar Sava, was captured during the Erez crossing raid the day before on Oct 8th.

Palestinian sources released footage of an assault on the Fajja base on Oct 9th, showing fighters breaking in and executing all IDF personnel inside. The footage showed a large number of piling bodies of IDF soldiers, but the exact number of the fatalities remains unknown.

Palestine

As per Palestinian Health ministry in Gaza, 560 palestinians were killed, and almost all were civilians, including 91 children and 61 women, and at least 2,900 others were injured.[source?] The Israeli airstrikes seem to target vital civilian facilities and areas of high civilian concentration,[source?] and not Hamas positions per se, in what seems to be a deliberate effort at collective punishment towards the Gazan population, which is considered a severe war crime by all international laws.[23][24][[Doctors Without Borders stated that Israel attacked the Indonesian Hospital in the Gaza Strip, killing one nurse, one ambulance driver while injuring several others.[24] The Israeli airstrikes seem to target vital civilian facilities and areas of high civilian concentration,[25] and not Hamas positions,[source?] in what seems to be a deliberate effort at collective punishment towards the Gazan population, which is considered a severe war crime by all international laws.[25]]]

USA response

The USA has publicly stated that they stand with Israel. The have sent a task force of US warships and aircraft to the area to support Israel.[25][26] The USA has also sent munitions to Israel. The US is fearful that that the conflict could spread wider to other countries in the region.[27] They are also anxious over the taking of US citizens as hostages by Hamas[28]
