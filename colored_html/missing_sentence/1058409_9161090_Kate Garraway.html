Britain’s Got More Talent is spin-off based of Britain's Got Talent. The series is hosted by Holly Willoughby.[[The series is hosted by Kate Garraway and Jamie Bamber.]] The previously host was Stephen Mulhern.

The series was hosted by Stephen Mulhern. He was joined by various guests including Ant McPartlin, Dec Donnelly, Simon Cowell, Amanda Holden, Piers Morgan, David Hasselhoff, Micheal McIntyre, Alesha Dixon and David Walliams.
