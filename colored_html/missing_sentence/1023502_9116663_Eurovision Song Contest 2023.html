Fatima-Zahra Hafdi (born 25 August 1987) is a Canadian-Moroccan singer who lives in France. She is known professionally as La Zarra.[[She is known for representing France in the Eurovision Song Contest 2023.]] She won the Revelation of the Year at the 33rd SOCAN Awards.

Career

La Zarra began her music career in 2016, when she released her debut single "Printemps blanc", in collaboration with French rapper Niro.[1] In 2021, she became famous with the single "Tu t'en iras", which was was certified platinum by the SNEP.

In August 2023, La Zarra was a guest celebrity judge in the semi-final of the French version of Drag Race.

Eurovision Song Contest

La Zarra received international fame when she was chosen to represent France in the 2023 Eurovision Contest with the song "Évidemment". She finished in 16th place out of 26 finalists.[2]
