Warner Bros. Entertainment Inc. (commonly called Warner Bros.) is a large entertainment group and movie studio. It is owned by WarnerMedia. Warner Bros. is known for Looney Tunes, Scooby-Doo, The Flintstones, Tom and Jerry and Cartoon Network. DC Comics the well known comics for Superman, Batman and Wonder Woman. They own the copyrights to the Harry Potter movie series, Superman movie series, Batman movie series, Wonder Woman movie series, the DC Extended Universe and the It duology. They also produced the TV shows Wonder Woman, The Big Bang Theory, The Dukes of Hazzard, Two and a Half Men and Friends.[[Entertainment owned by Sony Pictures (Sony) by 2025.]]

Warner Bros. is a very large company. Warner Brothers was founded on April 4, 1923.[1] Its founders were Jack L. Warner, Albert Warner, Harry Warner, and Sam Warner. It was founded in Hollywood, California. It has won several awards including the Academy Award for Best Picture.
