Patricia Honor Richardson MBE (née Greene; born January 1931)[1] is an English actress. She is best known as the voice of Jill Archer on the BBC Radio 4 radio soap opera The Archers.[2]

Greene is one of the world's longest-serving actors in a soap opera, in any medium.[3]

Early life

Patricia Honor Greene was born in January 1931 in Allenton, Derby, England.[1][4] She is the youngest of two children of Agnes Greene (née Johnson) and Edward Greene, an engineer and former piano salesman.[5] She has an older sister.[6]

Greene and her family moved to Chester Green when she was around the age of seven. She worked as a ward orderly at the Derby Children's Hospital. She also worked as a secretary in the sheet metal factory of Hawk Industries.[4][[Greene made her broadcasting debut in 1957.[4]]]

Career

Greene joined the cast of the BBC Radio 4 radio soap opera The Archers in 1957. She is the long-standing voice of Jill Archer (née Patterson).[2]

Personal life

Greene married actor Henry George Selway in October 1959 in Maidenhead, Berkshire. They later divorced.[7] Greene married Cyril Austen Richardson in April 1972 in Windsor, Berkshire.[8] The couple had one son.[9] Green was widowed in November 1986.[10]

Honours

Greene was appointed Member of the Order of the British Empire (MBE) in 1997 for her services to radio drama.[11]

Greene recieved an honorary degree from the University of Derby as a Master of Arts in 2017.[4]
