In September and October 2023, every municipal council in Norway, is supposed to elect a mayor. Four years later, the municipal councils will elect mayors, again.

(Names were added to this page, as late as October 7.)

  • In regard to Alstahaug municipality, media says that there is a written[1] agreement, that makes Peter Talseth the new mayor [when voting will be done at the next council meeting].[2] Furthermore, the municipality is in Nordland (county).[[Alta. The 'Labour Party, the Liberal Party, Socialist Left Party, Red Party, Centre Party, and KrF had the votes that elected Monica Nielsen as mayor; The mayor is a member of the Labour Party.[3] Furthermore, the municipality is in Troms og Finnmark county (and from 2024 Finnmark will be the county).]]
    • No mayor has been elected (2023) in these municipalities: Alta, (municipality), Alvdal, Alver, Andøy, Aremark, Arendal, Asker, Askvoll, Askøy, Aukra, Aure, Aurland, Aurskog-Høland, Austevoll, Austrheim, Averøy, Balsfjord
  • Bamble. Progress Party and the Conservative Party had the votes that elected Jon Pieter Flølo as mayor.[3] Vestfold og Telemark is the county.
    • No mayor has been elected (2023) in these municipalities: Bardu, Beiarn, Bergen, Berlevåg, Bindal, Birkenes, Bjerkreim, Bjørnafjorden
  • Bodø. The votes from municipal-council members who belong to Conservative Party, the Progress Party, and Liberal Party, got the new mayor elected, Odd-Emil Ingebrigtsen.[4] More information: the mayor is a member of the Conservative Party. Furthermore, the municipality is in Nordland (county).
    • No new mayor yet: Bokn (municipality), Bremanger, Brønnøy, Bygland, Bykle, Bærum
  • Bø. Conservative Party and Progress Party had the votes that elected Sture Pedersen as mayor.[5][6] Nordland is the county.
    • No new mayor yet: Bømlo,Båtsfjord, Deatnu - Tana, Dovre, Drammen, Drangedal, Dyrøy, Dønna, Eidfjord, Eidskog, Eidsvoll, Eigersund, Elverum, Enebakk, Engerdal, Etne, Etnedal, Evenes, Evje og Hornnes, Farsund
  • Fauske. Labour Party, Centre Party, Socialist Left Party; Marlen Rendall Berg became mayor.[7] She is a member of the Centre Party. More info: The Labour Party has 7 members on the council, the Centre Party has 4; The party that won the the local election (in Fauske), was the Conservative Party (26% of the votes), and it got 7 members on the council.[8] Nordland is the county.
    • No new mayor yet: Fedje, Fitjar, Fjaler, Fjord, Flakstad, Flatanger, Flekkefjord, Flesberg, Flå, Folldal, Fredrikstad, Frogn, Froland, Frosta, Frøya, Fyresdal
  • Færder. Conservative Party, Progress Party, Industry and Business Party Liberal Party, and Centre Party; Tom Mello.[9] Vestfold og Telemark is the county.
    • No new mayor yet: Gamvik, Gausdal, Gildeskål, Giske, Gjemnes, Gjerdrum, Gjerstad, Gjesdal, Gjøvik, Gloppen, Gol, Gran, Grane, Gratangen, Grimstad, Grong, Grue, Gulen, Hadsel, Halden, Hamar, Hamarøy, Hammerfest, Hareid
  • Harstad. Labour Party, Socialist Left, Red Party, Centre Party and Green Party; Kari Anne Opsal.[10] Troms og Finnmark is the county (as of 2023).
    • No new mayor yet: Hasvik, Hattfjelldal, Haugesund, Heim, Hemnes, Hemsedal, Herøy (Møre og Romsdal), Herøy (Nordland), Hitra, Hjartdal, Hjelmeland, Hol, Hole, Holmestrand, Holtålen, Horten, Hurdal
  • Hustadvika. KrF, and Centre Party; Nils Christian Harnes.[11] Møre og Romsdal is the county.
    • No new mayor yet: Hvaler, Hyllestad, Hægebostad, Høyanger, Høylandet, Hå, Ibestad, Inderøy
  • Indre Fosen. Conservative Party, Centre Party; Oskar Småvik.[12] Trøndelag is the county.
    • No new mayor yet: Indre Østfold, Iveland, Jevnaker, Karasjok, Karlsøy, Karmøy, Kautokeino, Kinn, Klepp, Kongsberg, Kongsvinger, Kragerø, Kristiansand, Kristiansund, Krødsherad, Kvam, Kvinesdal, Kvinnherad, Kviteseid, Kvitsøy, Kvæfjord, Kvænangen, Kåfjord, Larvik, Lavangen, Lebesby, Leirfjord, Leka, Lesja, Levanger, Lier, Lierne, Lillehammer, Lillesand, Lillestrøm, Lindesnes, Lom, Loppa,, Lund, Lunner, Lurøy, Luster, Lyngdal, Lyngen, Lærdal, Lødingen, Lørenskog, Løten, Malvik, Marker, Masfjorden, Melhus, Meløy, Meråker, Midt-Telemark, Midtre Gauldal, Modalen, Modum, Molde, Moskenes, Moss, Målselv
  • Måsøy. Bygdelista, the Conservative Party, and the Centre Party; Ingrid Majala.[13] Troms og Finnmark is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: Namsos, Namsskogan, Nannestad, Narvik, Nes, Nesbyen, Nesna, Nesodden, Nesseby, Nissedal, Nittedal, Nome, Nord-Aurdal, Nord-Fron, Nord-Odal, Nordkapp, Nordre Follo, Nordre Land, Nordreisa, Nore og Uvdal, Notodden, Nærøysund, Oppdal, Orkland, Os, Innlandet, Osen, Norway, Oslo, Osterøy, Overhalla, Porsanger
  • Porsgrunn. Labour Party, Progress Party, Industry and Business Party,[14] Socialist Left Party, Centre Party; Janicke Andreassen.[15] Telemark og Vestfold is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: Rakkestad, Rana
  • Randaberg. Centre Party, KrF; Jarle Bø.[16] Rogaland is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: Rauma, Rendalen, Rennebu, Rindal, Ringebu, Ringerrike, Ringsaker, Risør, Rollag, Rælingen, Rødøy, Røros, Røst, Røyrvik, Råde, Salangen, Saltdal, Samnanger, Sande, Sandefjord, Sandnes, Sarpsborg, Sauda, Sel, Selbu, Seljord, Senja, Sigdal, Siljan, Sirdal, Skaun, Skien, Skiptvet, Skjervøy, Skjåk, Smøla, Snåsa - Snåase, Sogndal, Sokndal, Sola, Solund, Sortland, Stad, Stange, Stavanger
  • Steigen. Socialist Left Party, Labour Party; Aase Refsnes.[17] Nordland is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: Steinkjer, Stjørdal, Stor-Elvdal, Stord, Storfjord, Strand, Stranda, Stryn, Sula, Suldal, Sunndal, Sunnfjord, Surnadal, Sveio, Sykkulven, Sømna, Søndre Land, Sør-Aurdal, Sør-Fron, Sør-Odal, Sør-Varanger, Sørfold, Sørreisa, Tana, Time, Tingvoll
  • Tinn. Labour Party, Conservative Party, Socialist Left Party; Kathrine Kvåle Haatvedt.[18] Vestfold og Telemark is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: TjeldsundTokke, Tolga, Tromsø, Trondheim, Trysil, Træna, Tvedestrand, Tydal, Tynset, Tysnes, Tysvær
  • Tønsberg. Conservative Party, Progress Party, KrF, Liberal Party; Frank Pedersen.[19][20] Vestfold og Telemark is the county.
    • No new mayors yet: Ullensaker, Ullensvang, Ulstein, Ulvik, Utsira, Vadsø, Vaksdal, Valle, Vang, Vanylven, Vardø, Vefsn, Vega, Vegårshei, Vennesla, Verdal, Vestby, Vestnes, Vestre Slidre, Vestre Toten, Vestvågøy, Vevelstad, Vik (municipality), Vindafjord, Vinje, Volda, Voss, Værøy, Vågan, Vågå, Våler (Innlandet), Våler (Østfold), Øksnes, Ørland, Ørsta, Østre Toten, Øvre Eiker, Øyer, Øygarden, Øystre Slidre, Åfjord, Ål, Ålesund, Åmli, Åmot, Årdal, Ås, Åseral, Åsnes
