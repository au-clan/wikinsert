The 16th century was a century in the Anno Domini and Common Era in the Julian and Gregorian calendars. It began on January 1, 1501 and ended on December 31, 1600.[[The year 1600 was the first century leap year in the Gregorian calendar.]] It is distinct from the century known as the 1500s which began on January 1, 1500 and ended on December 31, 1599. In this century, many Europeans visited or moved to the newly-found Americas and some also searched for new routes to Asia. There was much change in Europe, such as the Protestant Reformation.
