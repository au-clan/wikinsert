Suzanne Somers (October 16, 1946 – October 15, 2023) was an American actress, singer and business woman. She was born in San Bruno, California.

In 1977, she married Canadian producer Alan Hamel.

She appeared in many television series. Somers is best known for playing the part of Chrissy Snow on the first five seasons of Three's Company. She left due to a contract dispute with ABC.[[She was fired by ABC after she want to be paid the same as her male co-star John Ritter[1].]] She later made her comeback with the shows She's the Sheriff (which was syndicated) and Step by Step (which aired on ABC). She has written many bestselling books.

Somers was diagnosed with stage 1 breast cancer in 2000.[1] She had a lumpectomy and recovered.[1] She was criticized for her strong views about medical subjects. Her promotions of alternative cancer treatments had been subject to criticism from American Cancer Society.

On October 15, 2023, Somers died at the age of 76.[2]
