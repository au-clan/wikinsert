In mathematics, the quaternion number system extends the complex numbers.[[They were first described by Irish mathematician William Rowan Hamilton in 1843.[1][2] They are often used in computer graphics to compute 3-dimensional rotations.]] They were first described by Irish mathematician William Rowan Hamilton in 1843.[1][2]
