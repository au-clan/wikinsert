Guy Damien "The Flower"/"Le Démon Blond" Lafleur OC, CQ (September 20, 1951 – April 22, 2022) was a Canadian retired professional ice hockey right winger. Lafleur became the first player in the National Hockey League to score 50 goals and 100 points in six straight seasons. Lafleur spent 17 seasons in the NHL between the Montreal Canadiens, New York Rangers and Quebec Nordiques. During his time in the NHL, he won five Stanley Cups.[[He went by the nicknames "The Flower" and "The Demon Blond".]]

He was inducted into the Hockey Hall of Fame in 1988.[1]

Lafleur died on April 22, 2022 in Montreal, Canada from lung cancer, aged 70.[2]
