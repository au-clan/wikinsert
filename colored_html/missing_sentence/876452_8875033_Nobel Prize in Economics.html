Claudia Goldin (born May 14, 1946) is an American economic historian and labor economist.

She is the Henry Lee Professor of Economics at Harvard University. She is a co-director of the NBER's Gender in the Economy Study Group and was the director of the NBER’s Development of the American Economy program from 1989 to 2017.[[She win Nobel Prize in Economics in 2023.[1]]]

Goldin was the president of the American Economic Association between 2013 until 2014.[1]
