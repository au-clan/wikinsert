Tamim Iqbal Khan (Bengali: তামিম ইকবাল খান) (born 20 March 1989) is a Bangladeshi cricketer. Tamim made his One Day International and Test debut at 2007.[[He is a left-handed opening batsman and was the captain of Bangladesh national cricket team from March 2020 to August 2023.[1][2]]] He is left-handed opening batsman.
