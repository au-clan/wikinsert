Gori (Georgian: გორი [ɡɔri]) is a city in eastern Georgia. Gori is the regional capital of Shida Kartli.[[This City is best known for being the Birthplace of Soviet Dictator Joseph Stalin, who ruled the Soviet Union from 1924 until his death in 1953.]]
