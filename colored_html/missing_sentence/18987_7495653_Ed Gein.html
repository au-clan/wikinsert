The Texas Chainsaw Massacre is a 1974 American horror movie, based on real events.[[It was based loosely on real events, the crimes of Ed Gein, although much of its plot is fictional.]] It was directed by Tobe Hooper and stars Marilyn Burns. It is the first movie of The Texas Chainsaw Massacre franchise.

It was followed by several sequels and a 2003 remake from New Line Cinema.
