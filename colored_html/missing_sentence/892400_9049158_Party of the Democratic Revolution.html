Claudia Sheinbaum Pardo (born 24 June 1962) is a Mexican scientist and politician. She was the head of government of Mexico City from 2018 until 2023. She is the second woman and the first Jewish person to be elected Head of Mexico City.[1] She is a member of the National Regeneration Movement.[2][3][[During her time as a student at the National Autonomous University of Mexico, she was a member of the Consejo Estudiantil Universitario (University Student Council),[4] a group of students that would become the founding youth movement of the Party of the Democratic Revolution (PRD).[5]]]

She was the Secretary of the Environment of Mexico City from 2000 to 2006 during Andrés Manuel López Obrador's term as mayor. She was the Mayor of Tlalpan from 2015 to 2017.[4][5]

In 2023, she was listed as one of BBC's 100 Women.[6] Sheinbaum was seen as a possible candidate for President of Mexico in the 2024 election.[7][8] In June 2023, she resigned as mayor to run for the nomination of the Morena Party for the presidential election.[9]
