Elizabeth Hoffman (born February 8, 1927)[1] is an American character actress. Hoffman was born in Corvallis, Oregon.[1] Hoffman is best known for her regular role as Beatrice Reed Ventnor, Swoosie Kurtz', Sela Ward's, Patricia Kalember's and Julianne Phillips' characters' mother, on the NBC drama series Sisters (1991-1996).[2][3] She also played the role of Catherine Langford in Stargate SG-1.[4][[Hoffman died on August 21, 2023 at her home in Malibu, California from natural causes, aged 97.[5]]]
