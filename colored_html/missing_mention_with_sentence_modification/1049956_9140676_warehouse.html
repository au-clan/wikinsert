Company Group "Fundamentalno.rf" (Russian: Группа компании Фундаментально.рф) is a A Russian group of companies operating in the field of development and construction[1], founded in 2010 in Moscow.[2], the company focuses on large construction projects in Moscow and the Moscow Oblast.[3][4]

History

Fundamental.rf Group of Companies was founded in 2010 by Glub Tikhonov.[5] At the beginning of its activity, the group of companies determined the benchmarks in the industry and began to provide services in the following areas: integrated solution of works at the stage of zero cycle, as well as works on monolithic construction of any level of complexity.[6][7]

In 2012, it was one of the top five largest companies in Moscow for foundation construction works for private houses.[8] In 2015, the company launched a full cycle of monolithic construction technology in private housing construction.[9] In 2017, the company expanded its services for the construction of office, warehouse and hotel facilities.[10]

In 2018, the company formed its own independent fleet of construction special equipment: hoisting mechanization, pile driving units, drilling machines, concrete pumping stations. In 2020, the companies accounted for 6.2% of work production among private house building in the Moscow Oblast.[11]

At the end of 2021, the company signed contracts with major city developers, and in particular for the capital's residential complexes. Since mid-2022, the city has entrusted work in the historical part of Moscow, including on the territory of the Moscow Kremlin.[12]

Activities

Fundamentally.rf group of companies conducts development activities in the field of residential and commercial construction. The companies in the Fundamentalno.rf ecosystem realize the following activities: construction of residential and commercial complexes under the common brand Fundamentalno.rf.[1]

In the crisis of 2014-2015, the share of private low-rise construction decreased, in connection with which the group of companies "Fundamentalno.rf" made a strategic decision to refocus on clients-developers of the middle segment related to the construction of commercial real estate warehouse, office, retail buildings.[In the crisis of 2014-2015, the share of private low-rise construction decreased, in connection with which the group of companies "Fundamentalno.rf" made a strategic decision to refocus on clients-developers of the middle segment related to the construction of commercial real estate warehouse, office, retail buildings.[15]]

By 2018, the private house building market recovered and the share of orders from Fundamental.rf Group increased in the ratio of 1/2. With the onset of the pandemic, economic activity in Russia fell sharply. The construction industry gained many advantages, including: lack of self-isolation, freedom of movement, permission to continue construction work.

During this period, many customers came to the market, who received a favorable mortgage rate and worked remotely at home. There was an active demand to improve their living conditions, which led to a surge of orders in the construction sector. Today, Fundamental.rf is working on orders for the construction of hotels, offices, warehouse complexes, and private residences.[13]

Every year the company pours 5000 - 6000 m3 of concrete. The turnover of rebar at the facilities reaches 600 tons per year. For the whole period of the company's operation more than 40 000 m3 of concrete was poured and more than 4,5 mln. kg of reinforcement was used. Group of companies "Fundamentalno.rf" took part in exhibitions and expert conferences of the construction industry, organized by the following companies: "Holzhaus" (Exhibition "Wooden Housebuilding/Holzhaus from 21 - 24 April 2011 in IEC "Crocus Expo" (Moscow), "Beautiful Houses" (2012), professional construction portal "Forumhouse" (2010-2020) "HOUSECONF" (2022).[14]

Building

  • 2010 — 2013: construction of foundations and basements for private houses in the cottage village of Pavlovo and Pavlovo-2 on Novorizhskoye highway
  • 2014: development of a private residence "Millenium park". 3200 square meters were built. Construction took place on the zero cycle and monolithic part
  • 2016: construction of a hotel complex in Balashikha, monolithic works
  • 2017: construction of the OBINS shopping and office center
  • 2015: development of the Fantasy Island residential complex. Saw the reconstruction of the building with an additional floor addition
  • 2020 — 2022: construction of a private residence "Gardens of MAYENDORF" of 5000 square meters in the village of Barvikha. Performed the full cycle of construction
  • 2020 — 2022: provided technical supervision of the construction of the Capital Towers Moscow City tower.
  • 2022 — 2023: carried out foundation works (pile foundation) in "I Love" residential complex

Number of employees

Number of employees 10 persons of management staff, 20 persons of engineering staff, 3 persons of supply, 5 persons of engineering and technical service, 5 persons of design engineers for KM, KZh, KD sections. 3 design engineers for engineering networks. Masters-workers 200 persons. The total number of personnel of Fundamentalno.rf Group of Companies is 250 people.[8]
