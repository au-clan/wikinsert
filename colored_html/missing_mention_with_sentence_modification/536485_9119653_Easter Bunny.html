Rob Paulsen (born 1956) is an American voice actor, singer and former movie and television actor. He has done many voice roles in various movies, television programs and video games. He is best known for his roles as Pinky from Pinky and the Brain, P.J. in A Goofy Movie and Goof Troop, Carl Sneezer from The Adventures of Jimmy Neutron Boy Genius, Raphael (1987) and Donatello (2012) from Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Plank from Ed, Edd n Eddy and Yakko Warner and Dr. Otto Scratchansniff from Animaniacs.
