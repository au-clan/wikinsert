A Fire Engine or Fire Truck/Fire Apparatus is a Vehicle that mainly responds to Fire Incidents. Fire Engines or Fire Trucks/Fire Apparatus can be used for other emergency calls. These can include Hazardous Materials Incidents,Technical Rescue Incidents and Emergency Medical Services incidents. The type of emergency situations Fire Engines or Fire Trucks/Fire Apparatus respond to depends on where they are located as well as their setup..

a Fire Engine or Fire Truck/Fire Apparatus takes firefighters from a Fire Station or Firehouse/Firehall to a fire or other incident. Fire Engines or Fire Trucks/Fire Apparatus use Emergency Lights,Electronic,Electric or Mechanical Sirens and Electric or Electronic Horns to warn people that they are coming down the road and to get out of the way. These devices have been used for years to warn people when they are coming down the road. Fire Engines or Fire Trucks/Fire Apparatus also use Radios,Computers,Telephones and other equipment to contact their dispatcher or other emergency services vehicles.

A Fire Engine or Fire Truck/Fire Apparatus has Tools and Equipment for all Emergencies including Fires,Hazardous Materials Incidents,Technical Rescue Incidents and Medical Emergencies.

History

In the Middle Ages, the only way to fight a fire was with buckets of water. In the 16th century, hand-powered pumps were invented. In the 19th century, the design of these pumps was improved. With them, horses could be used to take them to the location of a fire. Later, steam power was used to both run the pumps and move them to the fires. The first modern fire engine was invented in 1905 by Knox Automobile in Springfield, Massachusetts.

Types

There are several types of specialized fire service vehicles. They include:

  • Ladder Company or Truck Company: A Ladder Company or Truck Company is used in Large Municipal Fire Departments.[Ladder company or Truck company: Ladder or truck companies are found in large municipal fire departments.] There are Several Ladder Company and Truck Company designations and they include Tower Ladder Companies,Aerial Tower Companies,Hook and Ladder Companies,Etc. Some Ladder Companies and Truck Companies use Quint Trucks if needed.
  • Water tender: A water tender is a vehicle which carries large amounts of water.
  • Heavy Rescue Companies: Heavy Rescue Companies use trucks that are supplied with a large variety of Specialized Tools and Equipment that deal with Heavy Rescue Situations. These include Motor Vehicle Accidents, Collapsed Buildings and other Situations. The Jaws Of Life Hydraulic Rescue Tool Systems (Spreaders,Cutters,Combi Tools,Rams,Etc),Air Bags,Rescue Saws,Air,Electric and Battery Operated Power Tools,Generators,Cribbing,Rope Rescue Equipment,Emergency Medical Services Equipment and other Specialized Tools and Equipment are found on a Heavy Rescue Company Apparatus.
  • Hazardous Materials Company: A Hazardous Materials Company uses a Specialized Vehicle that has Tools and Equipment on board to deal with Chemical,Biological,Radiological,Nuclear and High Yield Explosives (CBRNE) Incidents,Train Derailments and other Incidents. Hazardous Materials Protection Equipment,Non Sparking Tools and other Tools and Equipment are carried on a Hazardous Materials Company Apparatus.
  • Airport crash tender: An airport crash tender uses a vehicle that has special tools and equipment that deal with emergencies at large airports.
  • Ambulances and Medic Units: Ambulances and Medic Units utilize vehicles that are used for Medical Emergencies. They provide Services at Paramedic/Advanced Life Support and Emergency Medical Technician/Basic Life Support Levels. Emergency Medications,Cardiac Monitors and Defibrillators,Oxygen Equipment,Intubation and Airway Supplies and other items are on Ambulances and Medic Units. Ambulances use vans or box style units while Medic Units use utility vehicles or vehicles that have compartments that have medical equipment or they use vans or box vehicles just like Ambulances.
  • Chief Officer Vehicles: Large Municipal Fire Departments have Chief Officers in their Ranks. Chief Officers include Battalion Chiefs,District Chiefs,Division Chiefs,Assistant Chiefs and other Chief Officers. Radios,Telephones,Computers and other equipment are found in Chief Officer Vehicles. These vehicles respond to Major incidents and provide Command and Control Operations at these incidents.
  • Light and Air Unit: Light and Air Units provide power generation for any electric items that may be used on fire or rescue scenes and also have a system that fills up the Self Contained Breathing Apparatus that firefighters have on fire scenes.
  • Squad Company: A Squad Company utilizes either a Rescue Pumper or Utility Vehicle or in some areas a vehicle that resembles a Medic Unit or Rescue Ambulance.
  • Mobile Command and Communications Units: Mobile Command and Communications Units are found in Large Municipal Fire Departments. Senior Fire Personnel,Police Personnel and Emergency Medical Services Personnel can communicate with their dispatcher and other emergency services units. Computers,Teleprinters,Telephones,Computers and other equipment are located on a Mobile Command and Communications Unit.
