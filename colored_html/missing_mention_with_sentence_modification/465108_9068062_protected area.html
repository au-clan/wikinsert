Cabo de Gata-Níjar Natural Park is in the southeastern corner of Spain. It is Andalucia's largest coastal protected area.[It is Andalusia's largest coastal protected area.] It is a wild and isolated landscape with some of Europe's most original geological features. It is the only region in Europe with a Warm Desert climate.

Cabo de Gata-Níjar was one of 100 finalists for the 12 Treasures of Spain in 2007.[2]
