Taapsee Pannu is an Indian actress and model. who primarily works in Hindi, Telegu and Tamil language movies.[who primarily works in Hindi, Telugu and Tamil language movies.] She made her movie debut in 2010 by romantic musical movie Jhummandi Naadam and made her Hindi movie debut in 2013 by slapstick comedy movie Chashme Baddoor.
