Pietro Ubaldi (Milan,1950) is an Italian voice actor and singer and comedian.[Pietro Ubaldi (Milan, September 21 1955) is an Italian voice actor and singer and comedian.]

Biogaphy

He is the italian voice of Patrick Star from Spongebob, Doraemon from Doraemon and Reinhardt from Overwatch.
