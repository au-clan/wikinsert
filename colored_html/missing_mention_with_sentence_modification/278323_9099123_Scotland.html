Dexter's Laboratory (commonly abbreviated as Dexter's Lab) is an American science fiction animated television series. It was created by Genndy Tartakovsky for the Cartoon Network, as the first Cartoon Cartoon. The series was broadcast from 1996 to 2003. The series takes place in the fictional city of Genius Grove, California, and follows a red-haired boy named Dexter who had a secret laboratory filled with large number of his inventions. His inventions are normally destroyed by his older sister, Dee Dee. This causes Dexter to have to recreate his inventions. The series was followed by a Spin-off and related media. There has also been a Spin-off series titled The Beau Montgomery Show premiered on September 17, 1999 to November 7, 2003, a feature movie, Dexter's Laboratory: Ego Trip (1999), and also an upcoming reboot, of the same name, in late 2024.

Characters

  • Dexter McPherson (born March 25, 1988) is a red-haired genius. He works on inventions in his top-secret laboratory. His inventions help himself or the world. Dexter's inventions are usually smashed by his sister, Dee Dee. Dexter also keeps his lab a secret from his clueless parents. Dexter is motivated and inspired by Albert Einstein and other geniuses. Dexter has glasses, red hair, a lab coat and gloves and boots. He has his own lab pet monkey named Monkey (voiced by Frank Welker). The character was voiced by Christine Cavanaugh and Candi Milo.
  • Dee Dee McPherson (born June 22, 1984) is Dexter's sister. She is a tall blonde-haired girl. Dee Dee is high-spirited but not as smart as Dexter. She dances around Dexter's lab. Dee Dee usually plays around with Dexter and crushes his inventions. Dee Dee is interested in ballet and unicorns. She wears a short pink skirt and ballet shoes. Dee Dee is voiced by Allison Moore and Kat Cressida.
  • Mrs. McPherson (Mom) (born September 6, 1957) is a loving but clueless red-haired woman who is unaware of Dexter's secret laboratory. She is a stereotypical mother, a mysophobe with OCD who has trouble being around the rest of the family without her rubber gloves. Mrs. McPherson (Mom) is voiced by Kath Soucie.
  • Mr. McPherson (Dad) (born May 4, 1956) is a workaholic but also clueless blonde-haired man who is unaware of Dexter's secret laboratory. He is a stereotypical father, he loves bowling, golf, fishing, working in an office and has an unnatural affinity for his wife's muffins. Mr. McPherson (Dad) is voiced by Jeff Bennett.
  • Mandark Astronomonov is Dexter's black-haired arch-nemesis and rival. He is a brilliant scientist and fellow classmate who is always trying to one-up Dexter. He is also a very cruel and sadistic person who enjoys making Dexter's life miserable. Mandark has a large head and a small body, and he wears a red and black outfit. He is voiced by Eddie Deezen.
  • Beau Montgomery (born December 16, 1983) is Dee Dee's Canadian love interest and crush. He is a kind and gentle boy who is always there for Dee Dee. He is also very intelligent and creative. Beau is interested in science and Albert Einstein. He is best friends with Dexter, and the two of them often work together to solve problems. Beau is a loyal friend and always has Dexter's back. He has his own stereotypical parents (voiced by Kath Soucie and Maurice LaMarche) from Toronto, Ontario, Canada and his grandfather (voiced by Clive Revill) is from London, England.[He has his own stereotypical parents (voiced by Kath Soucie and Maurice LaMarche) from Toronto, Ontario, Canada and his grandfather (voiced by Brian Cox) is from Edinburgh, Scotland.] He has his own three pets, including a two Siberian Husky dogs named Sparky and Shadow and a Siamese cat named Mittens (were voiced by Frank Welker). He is voiced by Pamela Adlon and his singing voice was provided by teen singer Justin Sterling.

Critical reception

The series was one of Cartoon Network president Betty Cohen's favorite animated programs.[1] In an August 2002 Billboard interview, rapper Coolio said that he is a fan of the series. He states, "I watch a lot of cartoons because I have kids. I actually watch more cartoons than movies."[2]

Upcoming reboot series

The series was called Dexter's Laboratory reboot of the 1996 series, and developed by Nico Colaleo, Daisy Schofield and Alicia Chan, produced by Cartoon Network Studios and animated by Sunmin Image Pictures in South Korea, planned in early 2020, during production and resolved in 2023 rewrote and foward to 2024, this parodic version of the original show as a straightfoward light-hearted nature of the original show, similar of the 2016 The Powerpuff Girls.
