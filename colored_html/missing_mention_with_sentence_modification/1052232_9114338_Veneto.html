On 3 October 2023, a bus fell and crashed from an overpass in Mestre, Venice, Italy.[On 3 October 2023, a bus fell and crashed from an overpass in Mestre, Venice, Veneto, Italy.] The crash killed 21 people and 18 injuries.[1]

Crash

The crash happened at 19:45 CEST, when a bus was taking tourists from Piazzale Roma to a campsite in the Marghera district.[2][3] Witnesses reported that the bus scraped against a guardrail on a downhill section of a road leading from Mestre to Marghera, traveled about 50 meters, and then broke through the barrier. The bus fell about 15 meters, hit some electrical lines, and ended up upside-down near the Venezia Mestre railway station tracks, where it caught fire.[4]
