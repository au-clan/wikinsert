Christopher Mason Savino (born October 2, 1971) is an American cartoonist, animator, director, writer, storyboard artist, comic book artist and producer. He is well-known as the creator of the animated series The Loud House. Savino has also worked on The Ren & Stimpy Show, Garfield and Friends, Rugrats, Timon and Pumbaa, Animaniacs, Dexter's Laboratory, The Powerpuff Girls, Kick Buttowski: Suburban Daredevil, My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, Mickey Mouse, The Owl House, and The Ghost and Molly McGee.[Savino has also worked on A Pup Named Scooby-Doo, Tiny Toon Adventures, The Ren & Stimpy Show, Garfield and Friends, Rugrats, Timon and Pumbaa, Animaniacs, Dexter's Laboratory, Bobby's World, The Powerpuff Girls, Kick Buttowski: Suburban Daredevil, My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, Mickey Mouse, The Owl House, Hazbin Hotel, Helluva Boss and The Ghost and Molly McGee.]
