Amie Doherty (Born 1994) is an Irish composer and orchestrator for film and television.

Career

Before moving to the United States of America in 2013, Amie lived, worked and studied music in London, Ireland South Korea, Vietnam, Japan, India, and Spain. She graduated magna cum laude from Berklee College of Music's Masters program in Scoring for Film, Television, and Video Games, where she was awarded the Howard Shore Scholarship. later moved to Los Angeles where she went to USC Thornton School of Music and graduating in 2015 composing for video games and feature films.

In 2016, Amie became a Sundance Composer Fellow, having been chosen from over 500 composers to attend the intensive Sundance Institute Music & Sound Design Lab at the legendary Skywalker Sound. Following her time at the lab, she was announced as the 2016 Sundance Institute/Time Warner Foundation Composer Fellow.
