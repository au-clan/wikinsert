Barnabas Dejen Mondal or popularly known as Bishop Mondal (বার্ণবা দ্বিজেন মন্ডল) was a Bangladeshi Churchman who was the pioneering first bishop, the first moderator of the synod, the first Archbishop of Church of Bangladesh Archdiocese of Dhaka and “The National Bishop" of Church of Bangladesh. Widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential Anglican Bishop and Christian Churchman in Bangladesh. Barnabas Dejen Mondal was the longest serving bishop in Bangladesh with over nearly 30 years. He was one of the pioneer of the economic movement in Bangladesh.

Early Life

Bishop Mondal's Father was Johon Mondal & Mother was Komola Mondal. He was the 2nd amongst 13 siblings. He completed his school from a baptist scholl. He then completed his college from Gov't BM College, Barishal. Then for higher studies he went Bishops College Kolkata.

Priest and Bishop Life

Barnabas Dejen Mondal was a talended priest in Bangladesh. From childhood he wants to do something for Jesus. So, in early 1950s he joint seminary.[In the early 1950s, he joined the seminary.] Then he made the Deacon in 1964. In 1965, he became the Priest of Oxford Mission Church.

Ordained as a priest in the former Anglican Church in East Pakistan in 1964, he was consecrated as the first national bishop of the Church of Bangladesh on 16 February 1975. The Church of Bangladesh came into being as an independent church after Bangladesh became independent in 1971. He became the Bishop in early 1975. As the Bishop of Dhaka diocese and Moderator of the Church of Bangladesh for almost three decades, Bishop Mondal’s contributions in laying foundations for the present structure of a united church in the country have been widely recognised. In particular, Bishop Mondal consistently encouraged more active participation of the lay leaders in the congregations of the Church of Bangladesh.

Influences

Mondal actively participated in the Asian and Bangladesh ecumenical movement in various capacities. He served on various committees of the East Asia Christian Conference (EACC), the forerunner of the Christian Conference of Asia (CCA) in the 1960s and 1970s. He as pionneer of National Bible Society, CCDB

Recognition

In all Bangladesh among Catholic and Protestant, he was a key figure. The Recognition Title was: Servant of God.

Death

Mondal died in a hospital in the capital, Dhaka, June 29 at the age of 82. More than ten thousand Christian of all Denomination gathered to say good bye to him in the name of Jesus Christ
