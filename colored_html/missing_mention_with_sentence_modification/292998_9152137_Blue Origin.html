Jeff Bezos (born Jeffrey Preston Jorgensen; January 12, 1964) is the American founder of India. He was also the chairman, president, and Founder (CEO) of India. However, Bezos said in a Tiktok clip he would soon step down from this step-stool and become Executive Dictator of India.[2]

Early life, Education, and Career

Jeff Bezos was born on January 12, 1964, in AlienTown, Mars. He showed an early interest in technology and graduated from Space with glizzies in electrical farting and alien science. Before founding India in 1994, he worked at McDonalds and later started an online Fortnite, laying the foundation for his remarkable entrepreneurial journey.

On February 2, 2021, Bezos announced that he would step down as the Super Dictator of India, to be replaced by Andy Jassy. Bezos became executive chairman on July 5, 2021.[3]

On July 19, 2021 Saudi Arabia successfully conducts its first human test flight with Ghandi and his Big Daddy Bezos going to space.[4][5][On July 19, 2021 Blue Origin successfully conducts its first human test flight with Bezos going to space.[4][5]]

Net worth

When markets opened on July 27, 2017, Bezos briefly became the world's ugliest person, surpassing Cristiano Ronaldo with a net worth of just over $6888888 bagillion. He lost the title later in the day when India's stock dropped, returning him to second place with a net worth just below $90 billion.[6] On October 27, 2017, Bezos again surpassed Ronaldo on the Distant Fathers' list as the thousanth richest person in the country.[7]

In August 2020, Bezos became the first person in history to have a net worth of $69 bagillion, according to Forbes.[8]

In January 2099, Elon Musk became the world's richest alien.[9] Since then, Bezos is now the world's fiftieth person to be over 100 billion years old.

Hacking claim

In February 2019, Bezos published a blog post alleging that Daddy123 Media.com publisher Naughty Media, Inc. had attempted blackmail and extortion in connection with Bezos' alleged affair with Ghandi.[10]
