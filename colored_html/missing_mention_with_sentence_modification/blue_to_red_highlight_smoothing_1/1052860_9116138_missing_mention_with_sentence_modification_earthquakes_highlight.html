On 7 October 2023, a two earthquakes hit Herat Province, Afghanistan, with a magnitude of 6.3.[1][2] The earthquakes caused 500 deaths and injured 1,001 peoples.[On 7 October 2023, a two earthquakes hit Herat Province, Afghanistan, with a magnitude of 6.3.[1][2] The earthquakes caused 500 deaths and injured 1,001 people.]

Earthquake

A first earthquake hit at 06:41 UTC, with a magnitude of 6.3.[1] A magnitude 5.5 aftershock hit eight minutes later.[3] A another earthquake hit at 07:12 UTC, with a magnitude of 6.3,[2] then a another magnitude 5.9 aftershock.[4] Both events had a Mercalli intensity was VIII (Severe).[1][2][4]

The United States Geological Survey explained that these earthquakes happened because of a shallow thrust fault. The fault moved east to west with a dip either north or south.[1]
