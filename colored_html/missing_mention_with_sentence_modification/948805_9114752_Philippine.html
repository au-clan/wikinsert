Philippine Collective Media Corporation (PCMC: officially known in their documents as Philippine Collective Media Corporation) is a Philippine broadcast media company.[Philippine Collective Media Corporation (PCMC: officially known in their documents as Philippine Collective Media Corporation) is a Philippine television and radio broadcasting company.] It's headquarters located in Makati, with offices in Fairview, Quezon City and province in Tacloban. It owns a number of radio stations under the FMR (Favorite Music Radio) network as well as its own television station in Tacloban.
