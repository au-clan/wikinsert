Divine Ikubor better known as Rema is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter.[Divine Ikubor (born 1 May 2000), better known as Rema, is a Nigerian rapper, singer and songwriter.] He is signed to Jonzing Record and Mavin's Record.[1]

Early life

Rema was born in Benin City, Nigeria, in 2000.[2][3]

Career

He started making music at a young age. He began releasing music online when he was just 18 years old. His first song, "Iron Man", was release in 2020. Rema got global recognition In 2022 when make a remix of his song Calm down which make him to the billboard 100 and earned him a billion streams on Spotify.[4][5][6][7]
