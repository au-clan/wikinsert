Gudetama is a character created by the Japanese company Sanrio.[1][2] The name "Gudetama" is a combination of the Japanese words for lazy (gudegude) and egg (tamago) meaning a lazy egg yolk.[Gudetama is a fictional character created by the Japanese company Sanrio.[1][2] The name "Gudetama" is a combination of the Japanese words for lazy (gudegude) and egg (tamago) meaning a lazy egg yolk.] Gudetama was created by Emi Nagashima. Gudetama became popular because many saw the character as relatable. Gudetama isn't as popular as Hello Kitty, but certainly profitable. Gudetama featured in a show in Japan from 2014 - 2020 and is the main character of Gudetama: An Eggcellent Adventure. Video games and comics also exist. There are branded airplanes and trains, and restaurants have served Gudetama-themed foods. The character has featured on apparel, stationery and toys.
