Warren County is a county in the U.S. state of Kentucky. As of the 2010 census, the population was 113,792,[1] making it the fifth-most populous county in Kentucky.[As of the 2020 census, the population was 134,554,[1] making it the fifth-most populous county in Kentucky.] Its county seat is Bowling Green.[2]

Warren County is included in the Bowling Green, KY Metropolitan Statistical Area. It is in the Pennyroyal Plateau and Western Coal Fields regions.
