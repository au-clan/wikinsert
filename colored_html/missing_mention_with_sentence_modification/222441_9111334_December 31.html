The second millennium was a millennium in the Anno Domini and Common Era in the Julian and Gregorian calendars. It began on January 1, 1001 and ended on December 31, 2000.[The 2nd millennium began on January 1, 1001 and ended on December 31, 2000.] It is distinct from the millennium known as the 1000s which began on January 1, 1000 and ended on December 31, 1999.
