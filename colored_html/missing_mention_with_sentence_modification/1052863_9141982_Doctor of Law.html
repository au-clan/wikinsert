Professor of Civil Law in Ukraine

Kostruba Anatoliy is a Ukrainian professor specializing in Civil Law of Eastern European countries [1]. He earned his Doctor of Laws degree in 2015 and became a Professor in 2019.[2] He's been teaching at Vasyl Stefanyk Precarpathian National University since 2015 and is also a Professor at Vilnius University as of 2022.[3][4][5][6]

Background

Anatoliy was born in Simferopol, Ukraine, in 1978. He graduated from VI Vernadsky Tavriya National University in 2000 and obtained his Candidate of Law degree in 2004.[7][8][9][10][He graduated from VI Vernadsky Tavriya National University in 2000 and obtained his Candidate of Law degree in 2004.[7][8][9][10] In 2015, he became a Doctor of Law.]

Career

Anatoliy worked as a lawyer, legal adviser, and served as a deputy in the District Council of Simferopol. In 2005, he started private legal practice and became a lawyer in 2007.[11] He's a member of the Ukrainian National Bar Association and involved in the Committee on Investment and Privatization.[12][13]

Anatoliy has been a researcher and educator since 2003 at VI Vernadsky Tavriya National University. He's a visiting professor at the University of Civil Law "Ukraine" and was invited to teach students in Vilnius University, Lithuania, in 2022.[14] In 2022, he also became a visiting professor at Bologna University, Italy, and from 2023, he's a contractual researcher at University Paris 1 Panthéon - Sorbonne, France.[15][16][17]
