The province of Iloilo, is a province in the Philippines located in the Western Visayas region.[Flag Seal Iloilo is a province in the Philippines located in the Western Visayas region.] Its capital and largest city is Iloilo City, the regional center of Western Visayas and administratively independent despite being grouped by the province.
