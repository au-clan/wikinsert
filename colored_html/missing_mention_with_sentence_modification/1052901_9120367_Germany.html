On 7 October 2023, Palestinian militant groups from Gaza led by Hamas launched an offensive against Israel in what it called as Operation Al Aqsa Flood. Thousands of rockets were shot at Israel. Militants attacked some of its southern cities and settlements. Attacks came by land, air, and sea. Hundreds of Israelis were killed, and dozens (Israelis) were kidnapped. The attackers also killed and kidnapped some foreigners.[13] An Israeli operation was launched in response to the attack which was called Iron Swords by the IDF.[14] The armed conflict is part of the Gaza–Israel conflict, which is part of the Israeli–Palestinian conflict.

Names

Some militant groups called the attacks, Operation al-Aqsa Flood ('operation the deluge'; amaliyyat ṭūfān al-ʾAqṣā).[15][16] Israel authorities called its (own) counteroffensive (or counter-attack) Operation Iron Swords (Mivtsa harvot barzel).[17] Some news agencies and commentators have called the ongoing conflict as the Third Intifada(related pages, First and Second Intifadas).[18][19][20] Others have called it the Sukkot War, after the festivity celebrated the day the attack started.[21][22] Many analysts and officials have called the first attack as "Israel's 9/11 moment".[23][24][25]

Deaths

Israel

At least 900 Israelis were killed,

At least 900 Israelis were killed (including 73 soldiers).[26][27]

The police commander of Rahat, was killed.[28] The IDF said that 44 of its soldiers were killed.[29] The commander of the Nahal Brigade, Colonel Yonatan Steinberg, was killed near Kerem Shalom.[30]

In Ashkelon,[31] at least 68 died.[32] Dozens of bodies were reportedly discovered at a place, in Re'im, where there had been a party event that was part of a music festival.[33]

The Battle of Re'im happened, and there was fighting at a military base there. Colonel Roi Levy was killed in the battle.[34]

Palestinian reports say that at least 4 Israeli captives/POWs were killed by the Israeli airstrikes though the claim could not be independently verified.[35]

At least 11 Americans (or citizens of the United States), have been killed.[36]


Palestine

687 palestinians were killed in Gaza, and a large part of those were civilians, including 140 children and 105 women.[37]

Doctors Without Borders said that Israel attacked the Indonesian Hospital in the Gaza Strip, killing one nurse, one ambulance driver while injuring several others.[38]

Dead foreigners and dual-nationals

Some foreigners have died. Some of those had nationality from more than one country.

  • from  Thailand, 12 people were killed during the conflict.[39]
  • from the  United States, 11 Americans (or people from the United States) were killed during the conflict.[40]
  • from    Nepal, 10 people were killed.[41]
  • from  Argentina, 7 people[42]
  • from  France, 8 people[43][44]
  • from  Paraguay, 2 people[45]

Kidnappings

At least 100 Israelis were taken prisoner by Hamas and moved to the Gaza Strip.[46]


Around 130-150 IDF soldiers[47] and civilians were taken as hostages.

Many of those hostages were mostly civilian children, elderly, and young adults; Many of those were taken at a rave party.[27]

Moving a hostage on Israeli land, is also kidnapping.

Some foreigners were captured. Some of those have nationality from more than one country.

  • from    Nepal, 17 people were captured during the conflict[41]
  • from  Thailand, 11 people were captured[39]
  • from  France, 1 person[43][48][from    Nepal, 17 people were captured during the conflict[41] from  Thailand, 11 people were captured[39] from  Mexico, 2 people[45] from  France, 1 person[43][48] from  Germany, 1 person[49]]


International response

Some countries have given criticism against the attacks in Israel.[who?] Some countries have given criticism against the attacks on Gaza.[who?]

United States

United States authorities has sent an aircraft carrier with its combat airplanes and escorting warships, to the area to support Israel.[49][50] The USA has also sent munitions to Israel.[51]

US authorities think that there is a chance that the conflict could spread to other countries in the region.[52] U.S. authorities are against the taking of U.S. citizens as hostages by Hamas.[53] U.S. officials think that there is a chance that Iran-backed forces in Syria and Lebanon may open additional fronts against Israel.[54] US President Joe Biden had a phone call with Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu on Sunday morning and expressed "deep sympathy" for the killed, missing, and wounded in Israel and (promised or) pledged his full support to Israel.[55]

Response to the warfare of October 2023

Response to the attacks on Gaza

The leaders of some countries have tried to find words to give criticism to parts of Israel's attack (or retaliation) against Gaza.

Norway's prime minister said that Israel has a right to defend itself; He added that "we must give emphasis[56] [or say that it is important] that the Israelis show consideration towards civilians" [in Gaza].

Analysis

Israeli airstrikes allegedly seem to target civilian facilities like hospitals,[38] mosques,[57] schools,[58] etc and areas of high civilian concentration,[59] and not Hamas positions,[source?] in what seems to be a deliberate effort at collective punishment towards the Gazan population, which is considered a war crime.[59]
