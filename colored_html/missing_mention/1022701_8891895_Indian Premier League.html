Arjun Tendulkar
Personal information
BornMumbai, Maharashtra, India
RoleBowler

Arjun Tendulkar is an Indian cricketer.[1] He is the son of legendary cricketer Sachin Tendulkar and has gained attention for his own cricketing skills. Arjun has shown promise as an all-rounder, contributing with both bat and ball.[2] He plays for Mumbai Indians in Indian Premier League.[3]--{Indian Premier League}
