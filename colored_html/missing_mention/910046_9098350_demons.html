Hazbin Hotel is an American adult animated musical series created by Vivienne "VivziePop" Medrano.[1][2] Its pilot was released to YouTube on October 28, 2019.

The series focuses on Charlie Morningstar, the daughter of Lucifer and princess of Hell, with an almost impossible goal: to help the demons of Hell learn how to be better and get into Heaven.[3][4]--{demons}

The series was picked up by the independent film company A24. The show's first season will come out in January 2024 on Prime Video, and a second season is being worked on.

Promise

Charlie Morningstar (voiced by Jill Harris), the princess of Hell, opens a hotel called the "Happy Hotel", where she wants to teach demons of Hell how to be better people, in the hopes of getting them into Heaven. Due to overpopulation, Hell goes through an annual purge once a year, where angels descend from Heaven and kill demons. Charlie finds this upsetting, and wants to find a more peaceful solution to the overpopulation problem.[5]

With the help of her manager and girlfriend, Vaggie (voiced by Monica Franco), and their first customer, pornographic film actor Angel Dust (voiced by Michael Kovach, pilot), she's determined to make her dream become a reality. However, her plan attracts the attention of the powerful "Radio Demon", Alastor (voiced by Edward Bosco), who, despite finding her belief in redemption foolish, wants to help Charlie run the hotel for his own amusement.[6] Alastor renames the Happy Hotel the Hazbin Hotel.

Characters

  • Charlie Morningstar[7][8] (voiced by Jill Harris,[9] singing voice by Elsie Lovelock (pilot)), the main character of the series. She is the princess of Hell and the daughter of Lucifer and Lilith. She is the founder of the Hazbin Hotel. Despite what most of the demons of Hell say, she thinks that redemption is possible for all demons.
  • Vaggie (voiced by Monica Franco[10]), Charlie's girlfriend and manager of the Hazbin Hotel. Despite her explosive temper, she tries to run the hotel with a level head.
  • Angel Dust (voiced by Michael Kovach[11] (pilot)), an adult movie star and the hotel's first patient. He often gets into trouble and disobeys the hotel rules.
  • Alastor (voiced by Edward Bosco,[12] singing voice by Gabriel C. Brown (pilot)), one of the most powerful Overlords of Hell. He is Charlie's business partner, choosing to work at the Hazbin Hotel solely for his own amusement.[13]
  • Husk (voiced by Mick Lauer[14]), ill-tempered bartender and receptionist at the Hazbin Hotel. He is an alcoholic, and he loves gambling.
  • Niffty (voiced by Michelle Marie, pilot[15]), cleaner at the hotel. She is hyperactive and spontaneous, and is obsessed with cleanliness and men.[16][17]
