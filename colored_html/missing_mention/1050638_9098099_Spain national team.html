Catalina Thomas Coll Lluch (born 23 April 2001), also known as Cata Coll, is a Spanish soccer player. She plays as a goalkeeper for FC Barcelona in the Liga F league and also represents the Spain national team.--{Spain national team} Cata Coll started playing soccer when she was 6 years old for a team called Sant Marçal. Initially, she played as a central defender but switched to being a goalkeeper when she was 11. She even played on boys' teams until she was 14. Then, she moved on to women's teams, starting with Athletic Marratxí and later Collerense. In her last season with Collerense, she played a crucial role in helping the team get promoted to the Iberdrola Challenge League. She performed so well that she caught the attention of top-tier clubs in the Primera División. Eventually, she decided to join FC Barcelona.
