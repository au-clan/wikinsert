Thelma Rogers (24 June 1924[1] – 13 January 2000)[2] was an English actress. She was known as the voice of Peggy Archer on the BBC Radio 4 radio soap opera The Archers between 1953 and 1962.

Early life

lThelma Rogers was born in Foleshill, Warwickshire, England on 24 June 1924.[1] Her father, Edwin Moody Rogers, was the Lord Mayor of Coventry from 1966 to 1967.[2] Her mothers maiden name was Gould.[1] Rogers grew up in Coventry.[2]

Rogers studied for two years in Cambridge to become a teacher. She later dropped her teaching studies and joined the Leicester Repertory Company for four years.[2]--{Leicester}

Career

Rogers began her career in April 1953,[2] when she joined the cast of the BBC Radio 4 radio soap opera The Archers, taking over the role of Peggy Archer from June Spencer.[3] She was spotted in the early 1950s by Tony Shryane, the first producer, and was originally recruited as Elsie Catcher, a schoolteacher. She left the serial in 1962 and Spencer returned to her position as Peggy.[2]

Rogers worked the stage after her departure from The Archers. She later joined the Perth Repertory Company.[2] She took a prominent role while in Scotland, Lily Taylor in the ITV soap opera Take the High Road. Rogers other television credits include Crossroads and Coronation Street.[2]

Personal life

Rogers died on 13 January 2000. She was 75. Her obituary was written by Norman Painting and published by The Guardian on 21 January.[2]
