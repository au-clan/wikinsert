Blijdorp zoo is full of animals. It is found in Rotterdam in the Netherlands. There is a house with ice where the penguins live. The zoo is home to dangerous and not so dangerous animals. The polar bears live outside most of the year so they are not always white. The zoo hosts a large walk through aquarium. The lions and other animals are fed by the zookeepers. The pink flamingoes that live there are pink because they eat lots of shrimp. There is a lot of poop in the zoo. It gets used for growing plants. The zoo is divided into different parts based on where the animals live in the wild. Irma, the oldest elephant in the Netherlands, recently died.--{elephant}
