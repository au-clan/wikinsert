Alta, or Áltá, is a municipality in Norway's northernmost county.--{municipality}

Settlements include the town of Alta.

Administration

In October 2023, a new municipal council will meet, and it elects a mayor (for 4 years).

  • 9 council members are from Progress party[1]
  • 7 from Labour party
  • 4 from Conservative party
  • 4 from Venstre (Norway)
  • 3 from Socialist Left party
  • 2 from Red party
  • 2 from Centre party
  • 1 from KrF
  • 1 from Konservativt
  • 1 from MDG
  • 1 from Norgesdemokratene

(35 council members were elected during the 2023 Norwegian local elections.)
