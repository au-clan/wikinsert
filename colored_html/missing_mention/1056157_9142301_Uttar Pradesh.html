Hridaya Shankar Singh is the first Vice-Chancellor of Maa Shakumbhari University in Uttar Pradesh.[1][2] Before this Prof. Singh retired as a professor from the Zoology Department of Chaudhary Charan Singh University.[3] He has been Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Controller of Examinations at Chaudhary Charan Singh University, and Director at Sir Chhotu Ram Engineering College.[4]--{Uttar Pradesh}
