The Temple of Athena Nike is a temple on the Acropolis of Athens. It was named after the Greek goddess, Athena Nike.--{Greek goddess} Built around 420BC, the temple is the earliest fully Ionic temple on the Acropolis. The temple measured only 8.27 meters in width and 5.64 meters in length, and it had four columns on the front and back sides.[1]
