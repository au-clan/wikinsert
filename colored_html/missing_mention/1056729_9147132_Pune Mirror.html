Sanjay Ghodawat is an Indian businessman. He is the founder and chairman of the Sanjay Ghodawat Group, a conglomerate with business interests in education, healthcare, energy, mining, aviation, hospitality, and real estate.[1][2] He is also the founder and president of Sanjay Ghodawat University.[3] The Times of India included him in the list of Most Powerful Leaders of 2022.[4]

Career

He began his career in 1988 by selling chewing tobacco in the states of Karnataka, Maharashtra, and Goa. In 1994, he established a company called Ghodawat Pan Masala Products to produce pan masala, which was sold under the brand name 'Star'.[3] In 1993, he founded the Sanjay Ghodawat Group, a conglomerate based in Kolhapur, Maharashtra that operates in various sectors including hi-tech agriculture, energy, realty, softech, aviation, textiles, marble mining, and consumer products. Under the brand name 'Star', the group offers a range of products such as Namkeen (salted savories), rice, salt, edible oils, turmeric powder, atta (flour), and jaggery. It also produces beverages and fruit juices under the brand names 'Fizzinga' and 'Frustar', in addition to running Ghodawat Retail.[5][3] In 2017, he started an airline called Star Air. This airline began flying in 2019 as part of the Indian government's UDAN plan.[6] In the financial year ending March 2021, Ghodawat Consumer Limited, a part of Sanjay Ghodawat Group that sells everyday products, secured ₹1,000 crore in revenue.[7] He owns and operates several companies in different domains through his SG Group, including Ghodawat Agro, Ghodawat Aviation, Ghodawat Consumer Products, Ghodawat Energy, Ghodawat Food, Ghodawat Mining, Ghodawat Realty, Ghodawat Softtech, Ghodawat Textiles, Sanjay Ghodawat University, and Star Air.[2] He also worked as the chairman of Jain International Trade Organisation (JITO).[8]

Work

Sanjay Ghodawat operates a charitable organization known as the Sanjay Ghodawat Foundation. Through this foundation, he engages in various acts of philanthropy.[2] In 2019, during severe flooding in the regions of Kolhapur and Sangli, the foundation responded by providing financial assistance. Ghodawat donated Rs 51 lakh to former Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis to aid drought-affected farmers. The foundation also initiated relief efforts, including the provision of food, clothing, and shelter to flood victims.[2] In the same year, Ghodawat donated Rs 51 lakhs to support the families of 44 army personnel who tragically lost their lives in the Pulwama terror attack.[9] During the COVID-19 pandemic, the Sanjay Ghodawat Foundation distributed free meals to over 5 lakh individuals and provided essential medical kits to those in need.[2]

Awards and recognition

In November 2021, he received the Ahimsa International Award from the Governor of Maharashtra, Bhagat Singh Koshyari, in Mumbai for his philanthropy[10] In July 2022, he was awarded the Maharashtra Leadership Awards by Pune Mirror and the awarded as given by the Maharashtra state governor, Bhagat Singh Koshyari.[11] He was featured in The Times of India's list of the "Most Powerful Leaders of 2022".[4] In 2018, the Poona Merchants Chamber honored him with the Ideal Businessman Award.[12][13]--{Pune Mirror}
