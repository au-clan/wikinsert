June Rosalind Brocksom CBE (née Spencer; born 14 June 1919)[1] is an English retired actress.--{actress} She is known as the long-standing voice of Peggy Woolley on the BBC Radio 4 soap opera The Archers.[2]

Spencer became a centenarian on 14 June 2019.[3] She announced her retirement on 8 August 2022, at the age of 103, after 79 years in the industry.[4]

Early life

June Rosalind Spencer was born on 14 June 1919 in Nottingham, Nottinghamshire,[1] as the only child[5] of Rosalind Mary Spencer (née Thorne) and William Spencer.[6] She was baptised on 3 August 1919 at St John's Church in Carrington, Nottingham.[7]

Spencer attended Mountfield House Preparatory School from 1924 to 1929. She graduated from Nottingham High School for Girls in 1934, at the age of 15. She joined the St Mary's Amateur Dramatic Society in Lace Market and gained a London Guildhall School of Music and Drama certificate.[8]

Spencer started piano lessons at the age of six,[8] passing the Associated Board’s Advanced Level.[5]

Career

Spencer made her broadcasting debut in 1943.[2]

Spencer joined the BBC Radio 4 soap opera The Archer in May 1950. She served as the voice of Peggy Woolley (née Perkins, formally Archer) for 67 years, from the pilot episode until her departure on 31 July 2022. She was the sole survivor from the original cast.[2] Spencer also portrayed other characters including a Scottish maid and an Irish baker's assistant called Rita Flynn. She once portrayed two of her characters in a single scene.[9]

Spencer is also a stage actress.[10]

Spencer portrayed Marian Hennessy on an episode of the BBC One soap opera Doctors in 2000.[11]

Spencer was the guest on the 28 February 2010 episode of the BBC Radio 4 programme Desert Island Discs.[12]

Personal life

Spencer first met Roger Sidney Brocksom at the age of 17.[5] The couple married in 1942 in Nottingham, Nottinghamshire.[13] They adopted two children, a son and a daughter.[14] Brocksom died in May 2001. He was 81.[15] He had been diagnosed with Alzheimer's disease in 1992.[5] Their son David died from alcoholism in 2006. He was 55.[14]

Spencer currently resides in a bungalow in Leatherhead, Surrey.[16] She also owns a house in Menorca, Spain.[5]

Spencer sponsors a child in Thailand who calls her 'Granny June'.[17]

Honours

Spencer was appointed Officer of the Order of the British Empire (OBE) in 1991 and Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in the 2017 Birthday Honours for services to drama and charity.[18]

Spencer received the Freedom of the City of London in June 2010.[19]

Spencer was awarded an honorary degree from the University of Nottingham as a Doctor of Letters for her services to broadcasting in July 2012.[20]

Spencer received the lifetime achievement award at the BBC Audio Drama Awards in January 2014.[21]

Spencer received the GDST Exceptional Contribution award from Nottingham Girls' High School in July 2022.[22]
