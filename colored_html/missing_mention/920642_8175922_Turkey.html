Crytek GmbH is a German video game developer and software developer based in Frankfurt. It was founded by the Yerli brothers in Coburg in 1999 and moved to Frankfurt in 2006, Crytek also have studios in Kyiv and Istanbul.[3]

Subsidiaries

  • Crytek Kiev in Kyiv, Ukraine — founded in 2006.
  • Crytek Istanbul in Istanbul, Turkey — founded in 2012.--{Turkey}
