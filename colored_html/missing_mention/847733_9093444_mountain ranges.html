Madongan Dam is a dam in Barangay San Marcelino, Municipality of Dingras, Ilocos Norte.[1] There is an access road to reach the dam.[2]

Tourists can to see the cold waters falling from Madongan River. A short hike gives a view of the mountain ranges around the area.--{mountain ranges}
