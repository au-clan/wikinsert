The Snuts are a scottish iindie rock band from West Lothian, Scotland. The lineup is made up of Jack Cochrane (vocals and guitar), Joe McGillveray (guitar), Callum '29' Wilson (bass) and Jordan 'Joko' Mackay (drums).
